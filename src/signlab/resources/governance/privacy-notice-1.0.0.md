## What is collected

With your separate consent choices, SignLab may collect identifiable video of your
hands, face, body, clothing, background, and surroundings. Audio is disabled.
Software may derive hand landmarks, body anchors, motion features, quality signals,
embeddings, predictions, and evaluation records. Random pseudonymous identifiers
link those research records. Pseudonymous data is not guaranteed anonymous.

Your name, contact details, signature, completed form, and the mapping to your
pseudonymous identifier remain in a separate encrypted identity vault. They must
not appear in research filenames, manifests, logs, datasets, experiments, models,
Git, DVC, or MLflow.

## Why and how data is used

Data is used only for the bounded SignLab five-gesture research purpose described
in the consent form and only for the permissions you grant for each recording.
Optional permissions cover derived features, training, evaluation, public display
of selected video, redistribution of raw media or derived features, aggregate
results, and trained model weights. SignLab does not authorize audio collection,
minor participation, identity inference, commercial sale, surveillance, or
participant-level public ranking in version 1.

Approved researchers receive least-privilege access to restricted research data.
The data steward operates the identity vault and withdrawal process. Release
reviewers receive only the provenance and permission evidence needed for review.
Service providers, storage locations, and recipients must be listed here before
collection: `{{APPROVED_SYSTEMS_PROVIDERS_AND_RECIPIENTS}}`.

## Retention, security, and withdrawal

The approved retention rule is `{{RETENTION_END_DATE_OR_RULE}}`, never longer than
24 months from capture for raw and derived participant data. Backup handling and
tested deletion are described at `{{BACKUP_AND_DELETION_DESCRIPTION}}`. Encryption,
access control, audit logging, and a separate identity vault reduce risk but cannot
guarantee that disclosure or reidentification will never occur.

You may stop participation or request complete withdrawal without penalty through
the contact above. Verified withdrawal freezes new use, traces affected descendants,
and schedules deletion, invalidation, retirement, retraction, or rebuilding. A
minimal pseudonymous tombstone may remain to prevent re-import. Copies already
downloaded by third parties after an authorized public redistribution may not be
fully recoverable.

You will receive this notice and a copy of the completed consent form. Ask the
